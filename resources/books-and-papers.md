# Books, Ebooks, and Research Papers

In-depth technical foundations, academic surveys, and system engineering texts.

| Work | Author / Institution | Direct Link | Intended Audience | Learning Value | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Engineering (Chapter 5: Prompt Engineering)** | Chip Huyen (O'Reilly) | [huyenchip.com/books](https://huyenchip.com/) · [oreilly.com](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) | Software Engineers building real AI apps | Treats prompt engineering as a disciplined component of software systems: evaluation sets, cost/latency tradeoffs, and safety guards. | Commercial book / O'Reilly subscription |
| **The Prompt Report: A Systematic Survey of Prompting Techniques** | Schulhoff et al. (Vanderbilt, LearnPrompting) | [arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608) | Researchers & Advanced Practitioners | Taxonomy of 58 prompting techniques covering reasoning, alignment, security, and multimodal prompting. | Open Access (arXiv) |
| **Google Prompt Engineering Whitepaper** | Lee Boonstra (Google Cloud) | [cloud.google.com/resources](https://cloud.google.com/) | Enterprise Architects | A concise 68-page practical reference covering foundational principles, context windows, and model steering. | Free PDF |
| **Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models** | Wei et al. (Google Research) | [arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903) | ML Engineers & Researchers | Foundational research paper introducing step-by-step reasoning prompts and few-shot exemplars. | Open Access (arXiv) |
| **Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with LLMs** | Yao et al. (Princeton, DeepMind) | [arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601) | AI Architects | Generalizes chain-of-thought to explore multiple reasoning paths with self-evaluation and backtracking. | Open Access (arXiv) |
