**Category:** Writing & Content

## Prompt

> Write a concise, compelling elevator pitch for a product or service.
> 
> Inputs:
> - Product/Service: ${product}
> - Target Audience: ${audience}
> - Core Problem Solved: ${problem}
> - Key Differentiator: ${differentiator}
> 
> Deliver:
> 1. A 30-second spoken pitch (under 80 words) with an attention-grabbing hook.
> 2. A clear 1-sentence tagline.
> 3. A 3-bullet summary covering problem, solution, and clear call-to-action.
