## Prompt

> Summarize what has been learned about me, my workflow, and my preferences from our conversation history and accessible memory.
> 
> Please organize into:
> 1. Stated background, role, and domain
> 2. Technical stack and preferred tools
> 3. Active projects and recurring tasks
